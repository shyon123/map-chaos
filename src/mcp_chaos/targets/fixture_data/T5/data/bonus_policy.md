# Bonus policy

The annual bonus is the employee's base salary multiplied by the bonus rate of
their department. Rates are percentages of base salary.

## 2024 rates (current)

| Department  | Bonus rate |
|-------------|-----------:|
| Platform    | 12%        |
| Data        | 10%        |
| Design      | 8%         |
| Support     | 6%         |

## 2023 rates (superseded, do not use)

| Department  | Bonus rate |
|-------------|-----------:|
| Platform    | 9%         |
| Data        | 9%         |
| Design      | 7%         |
| Support     | 5%         |
